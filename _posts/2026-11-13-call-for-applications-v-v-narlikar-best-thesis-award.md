<h2><a href="https://iagrg.in/vvn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">V. V. Narlikar Best Thesis Award</a></h2>

<p>
The <strong>Indian Association for General Relativity and Gravitation (IAGRG)</strong>
invites applications for the <strong>V. V. Narlikar Best Thesis Award</strong>,
to be presented at the forthcoming <strong>34th IAGRG Meeting</strong>, titled
<strong><em>Gravitation: Frontiers and Beyond</em></strong>, to be held during
<strong>January 7–9, 2027</strong>, at
<strong>St. Albert's College (Autonomous), Ernakulam, Kerala</strong>.
</p>

<p>
The award is presented at every IAGRG meeting to recognize the best Ph.D. thesis
on General Relativity and related areas that has been accepted by a university
or institute in India since January 2025. The award carries a citation and
prize money of <strong>Rs. 5,000/-</strong>.
</p>

<p>
Students who have recently completed their Ph.D. and meet the award criteria
are invited to submit their applications through the
<a href="https://forms.gle/HWrmAMhzyoiWion67" target="_blank" rel="noopener">V. V. Narlikar Best Thesis Award application form</a>.
</p>

<div class="notice-box">
    <h3>Important Dates and Award Details</h3>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Thesis eligibility:</strong> Ph.D. thesis accepted by a university or institute in India since January 2025</li>
        <li><strong>Research area:</strong> General Relativity and related areas</li>
        <li><strong>Award:</strong> Citation and prize money of Rs. 5,000/-</li>
        <li><strong>Application deadline:</strong> November 21, 2026</li>
        <li><strong>Application form:</strong> <a href="https://forms.gle/HWrmAMhzyoiWion67" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Form</a></li>
        <li><strong>Further details:</strong> <a href="https://iagrg.in/vvn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">iagrg.in/vvn</a></li>
    </ul>
</div>

<p>
Usually, three or four submissions are shortlisted for presentations at the
IAGRG meeting. The winner is selected by a committee constituted by the Local
Organizing Committee (LOC) of the meeting.
</p>

<p>
For queries regarding the V. V. Narlikar Best Thesis Award, please contact
<strong>Dr. Sudipta Das</strong> at
<a href="mailto:sudipta.das@visva-bharati.ac.in">sudipta.das@visva-bharati.ac.in</a>,
using the subject line <strong>"V V Narlikar Best Thesis Award"</strong>.
</p>
