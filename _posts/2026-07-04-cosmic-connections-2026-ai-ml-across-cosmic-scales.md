**Venue:** IMSc, Chennai

**Registration Deadline:** 7/25/2026

**Abstract Deadline:** 7/25/2026

Cosmic Connections 2026: AI/ML Across Cosmic Scales is the second edition of the Cosmic Connections national meeting, to be held at the Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc), Chennai, India. The three-day conference will take place from September 7 to 9, 2026, followed by a dedicated AI/ML workshop on September 10 and 11, 2026. The meeting aims to bring together researchers in cosmology from across India, with a focus on recent developments across the Early, Intermediate, and Late Universe, as well as the growing role of artificial intelligence and machine learning across cosmic scales.
