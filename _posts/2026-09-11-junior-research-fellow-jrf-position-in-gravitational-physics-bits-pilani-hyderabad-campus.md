**Location:** Hyderabad, India

**Deadline:** 20-09-2026

Applications are invited from motivated and qualified candidates for a Junior Research Fellow position under an early career ANRF grant in theoretical gravitational physics.

Job Description: A successful candidate will research on the project titled "Asymptotic symmetries and cosmological memory effect in FLRW space-times" under the supervision of Dr. Sk Jahanur Hoque. Selected candidate may be permitted to register for the PhD programme of BITS Pilani, Hyderabad campus subject to the fulfilment of the requirements.

Eligibility: First-class with M. Sc. in Physics with valid GATE/NET qualified candidates will be preferred. Candidates with good knowledge in General Theory of Relativity, mathematica, tensor manipulations are encouraged to apply. Age limit 28 years. 

Project position: JRF (1 position)
Duration of the project: 3 years
Fellowship amount: Rs. 37000/month + HRA (as per ANRF, India norms/ Institute norms)

The application should contain a SINGLE PDF with :
1. A detailed CV.
2. A research experience (if any, maximum 1 page)

Application procedure: All the documents may be sent directly to Dr. Sk Jahanur Hoque at <jahanur.hoque@hyderabad.bits-pilani.ac.in> with a subject line: `<Applicant Full Name> JRF2026’. 

Mode of selection: Only the short-listed candidates will be called for an online interview. The online interview will be tentatively conducted in the last week of September. The candidate will be selected based on the recommendation of a selection committee. 

Deadline for application: 20th September, 2026, 5 PM (IST)

Contact: For any queries regarding the position, contact Dr. Sk Jahanur Hoque at jahanur.hoque@hyderabad.bits-pilani.ac.in
