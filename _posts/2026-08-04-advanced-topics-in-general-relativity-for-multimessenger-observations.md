**Venue:** IUCAA

**Dates:** 10/5/2026 to 10/16/2026

**Registration Deadline:** 8/7/2026

**Abstract Deadline:** 8/7/2026

We are pleased to announce a new course on Advanced Topics 
in General Relativity for Multimessenger Observations, to be held at 
IUCAA between October 5 – 16, 2026. It will be a two-week 
intensive course on advanced General Relativity topics and tools 
relevant to current and upcoming missions. The course is open to 
college and university faculty members, post-doctoral fellows, and 
research scholars.

The detailed schedule and logistic arrangements will be emailed
to those selected well before the start of the course. Travel support
and free hospitality will be provided to all participants as per norms.
Participants should be present at IUCAA for the entire course duration.
Certificates will be provided based on attendance, participation, and
assessments. This course is equivalent to a Short Term Programme (STP) 
as per UGC guidelines.
