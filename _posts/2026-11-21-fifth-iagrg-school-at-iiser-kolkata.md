Indian Association for General Relativity and Gravitation (IAGRG) has been organizing schools on Gravitation and Cosmology for the last four years to systematically train budding researchers on the contemporary research topics in gravitational physics, cosmology and related areas. The participants of the school are expected to gain a broad and in-depth understanding about cutting-edge research areas, which will help them in progressing their research in these directions. Participants also get an opportunity to interact closely with the speakers, who are leading experts in their respective fields of research. The goal is to provide the participants with the desired exposure to the current state of the art research. The school is designed for extensive engagement, with tutorials and discussion sessions that aim to provide hands-on experience for the participants on how to tackle advanced research problems in these fields of research.  


The fifth IAGRG School will be held at the Department of Physical Sciences,  IISER Kolkata, from February 8 to February 19, 2027. The program will include four lecture series covering diverse topics in gravitation and cosmology, delivered by leading experts in the field.
