<h2><a href="https://iagrg.in/nrsen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">N R Sen Young Researcher Award</a></h2>

<p>
The <strong>Indian Association for General Relativity and Gravitation (IAGRG)</strong>
invites nominations for the <strong>N R Sen Young Researcher Award</strong>, to be
presented at the <strong>34th IAGRG Meeting</strong>, titled
<strong><em>Gravitation: Frontiers and Beyond</em></strong>, to be held during
<strong>January 7–9, 2027</strong>, at
<strong>St. Albert's College (Autonomous), Ernakulam, Kerala</strong>.
</p>

<p>
The N R Sen Young Researcher Award is presented every two years to a young
researcher in recognition of outstanding contributions to gravitational physics.
The award carries an honorarium of <strong>Rs. 10,000/-</strong> and a citation.
Further information is available on the
<a href="https://iagrg.in/nrsen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">award webpage</a>.
</p>

<div class="notice-box">
    <h3>Eligibility Criteria</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>The nominee must hold a doctoral degree and be an Indian national.</li>
        <li>The nominee must have spent at least four years working in India.</li>
        <li>As of December 31, 2025, the age limit is 40 years for men and 42 years for women who have availed of maternity/child-care leave or had career breaks.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>
    Please consult the attached <em>NRSENAward.pdf</em> for the complete terms
    and conditions of the award.
    </p>
</div>

<h3>Nomination Requirements</h3>

<p>
All IAGRG members are eligible to nominate a prospective awardee. A nomination
must include:
</p>

<ul>
    <li>A brief CV;</li>
    <li>A list of publications;</li>
    <li>A summary of past research and future plans;</li>
    <li>Two recommendation letters from individuals familiar with the candidate's work; and</li>
    <li>A cover letter from the nominator explaining why the candidate deserves the award.</li>
</ul>

<p>
<strong>Self-nominations are also permitted.</strong> In such cases, the candidate
must submit three recommendation letters. All other eligibility requirements
remain unchanged.
</p>

<p>
Please submit nominations through the
<a href="https://forms.gle/b6yYgT3QwPrDjfnC9" target="_blank" rel="noopener">N R Sen Young Researcher Award nomination form</a>.
</p>

<div class="notice-box">
    <h3>Important Dates and Submission Details</h3>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Nomination deadline:</strong> November 21, 2026</li>
        <li><strong>Nomination form:</strong> <a href="https://forms.gle/b6yYgT3QwPrDjfnC9" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Form</a></li>
        <li><strong>Award information:</strong> <a href="https://iagrg.in/nrsen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">iagrg.in/nrsen</a></li>
    </ul>
</div>

<p>
Nominees are requested to ask their recommenders to send recommendation letters
directly to <strong>Dr. Sudipta Das</strong> at
<a href="mailto:sudipta.das@visva-bharati.ac.in">sudipta.das@visva-bharati.ac.in</a>,
with the subject line <strong>"N R Sen Young Researcher Award"</strong>.
</p>
