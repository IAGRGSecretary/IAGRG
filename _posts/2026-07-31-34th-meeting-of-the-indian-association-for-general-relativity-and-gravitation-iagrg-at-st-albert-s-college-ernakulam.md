<h2>Gravitation: Frontiers and Beyond</h2>

<p>
The <strong>Indian Association for General Relativity and Gravitation (IAGRG)</strong>
is pleased to announce its <strong>34th Meeting</strong>, titled
<strong><em>Gravitation: Frontiers and Beyond</em></strong>,
to be held from <strong>January 7–9, 2027</strong> at
<strong>St. Albert's College (Autonomous), Ernakulam, Kerala</strong>.
</p>

<p>
The annual IAGRG meeting brings together researchers, faculty members,
postdoctoral fellows, and students working across gravitation, cosmology,
astrophysics, and related areas of theoretical and experimental physics.
The conference provides a forum to present recent advances, exchange ideas,
and foster collaborations within the Indian and international gravitation
community.
</p>
